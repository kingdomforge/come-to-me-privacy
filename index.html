Privacy Policy: Come to Me Publisher

Last updated: 6 November 2026

"Come to Me Publisher" is a small tool used by the Come to Me Prayer and Reflection ministry to publish its own prayer videos to its own Facebook Page. It is used only by the people who run that Page.

What the tool does

  • It posts the ministry's own videos, with their titles and descriptions, to the ministry's Facebook Page.
  • It reads back the posts it made, to check that they were published.

What information we collect

We do not collect, store, sell or share personal information about visitors, followers or any other person. The tool does not read comments, messages or the profiles of people who visit the Page.

The only data the tool holds is an access code for the ministry's own Page, kept privately on the computer of the person who runs the Page. It is never shown or shared.

Sharing

We do not share any information with anyone.

Deleting data

Because we hold no personal data about visitors, there is nothing to delete. If you believe we hold information about you, message the Come to Me Prayer and Reflection Facebook Page and we will answer you.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new date will appear at the top.

Contact
